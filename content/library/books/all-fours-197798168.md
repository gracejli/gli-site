notes: 
- musings on desire in the first half before she returns home is the most compelling part of this book to me
- what does it mean to be an artist trying to subject your life to experiences in the name of commodified, profitable art? your life as the subject? stretching your experience to emotional lows and highs, examining your moral reaction to taboo, fantasy, desire, cheating? 
- it is indulgent for a while to read someone, a woman, being perverse, but after a while it becomes difficult to follow along her actions. it almost feels like to continue to read you are complicit 
- reading this again, when older will be different experience
