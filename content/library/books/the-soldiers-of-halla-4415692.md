so much in the scifi/proposed societies of the 7 territories:
- denduron: society overrun by greed and mining, and technology unevenly distributed, monarchical 
- zadaa: society struggling over natural resources (water) 
- quillan: society overrun by gambling, capitalism
- veelox: digital escapism through lifelight 
- second earth: of course, the rise of fascism and otherness (with people unevenly given the chance for happiness)
