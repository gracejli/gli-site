read for bookclub. finished during memorial day camping weekend. is more like a short story, than anything. 

Wonderful to go into this book without any knowledge of what was inside to feel my brain wrap around the stories and how they all relate to each other. I felt confused, unsatisfied, when I first finished it, having entered into the book expecting something longer. The richness of the stories were cut short. 

Through talking about it with Lucia on the drive back on multiple occasions, I'm reminded that I don't read enough stories set in the 2020's, with stories and anecdotes that hold mirrors up to what is around me. And! more interestingly, these familiar stories are woven together, smartly, in a web of a short story: using the methods of modern technology and transcription to talk about something that cannot be described or captured well enough: the experience of seeing, the experience of feeling your own mind shift what you see, your effective "reality" and "truth", and how you remember it, and then how that story mutates with each retelling, and each visit again in your memory. What is fiction, is it fiction, if it is true to you? 

It was so quick - done in 2 hours, ill be reading it again before the bookclub discussion to sit with it again.
